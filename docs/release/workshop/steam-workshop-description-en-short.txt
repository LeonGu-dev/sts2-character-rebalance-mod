[h1]Character Rebalance[/h1]

Rebalances selected Ironclad, Silent, Defect, and Regent cards, and adds four new cards. Changes include stats, costs, rarities, and mechanics. Card text is available in English and Simplified Chinese.

[b]Game version:[/b] Slay the Spire 2 v0.111.0
[b]Numbers:[/b] Base/upgraded values. Every changed card is listed below with a brief summary.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Ironclad[/h1]

[b]Buffs[/b]
[list]
[*]Dark Embrace: Rare to Uncommon. Entrench: Event card added to the Ironclad pool as Uncommon.
[*]Dominate: base Vulnerable 1 to 2; upgraded cost 1 to 0.
[/list]

[b]Nerfs[/b]
[list]
[*]Ashen Strike: bonus damage per card in the Exhaust pile 3/4 to 2/3.
[*]Molten Fist: damage 10/14 to 8/12. Break: damage 20/30 to 15/20.
[/list]

[b]Mixed stat changes[/b]
[list]
[*]Colossus: Block 4/7 to 6/9; damage reduction against Vulnerable enemies 50% to 25%. Now Common.
[*]Crimson Mantle: upgraded Block 10 to 7, but it no longer costs 1 HP each turn when upgraded.
[/list]

[b]Mechanics and reworks[/b]
[list]
[*]Aggression: its upgrade changes from Innate to a cost reduction from 1 to 0.
[*]Brand: Strength 1/2 to 1/1; the upgrade also draws 1 card.
[*]Fight Me!: Strength 3/4 to 2/3; damage 5/6 to 5/5 twice. It now gains Strength before dealing damage.
[*]Corruption: its upgrade changes from a cost reduction from 3 to 2 to Innate.
[*]Drum of Battle: cost increases by 1 for the rest of the turn after each play. The upgrade starts at 0 cost; it no longer grants 2/3 energy when exhausted.
[*]Thrash: one hit of 8 base damage; the upgrade lets you choose the Attack to exhaust.
[*]Conflagration rework: costs 2 and exhausts Attacks in your hand. For each, deal 6/8 damage to all enemies and lose 1 HP.
[*]Forgotten Ritual rework: now an Attack dealing 8/11 damage. Gain 3/4 energy if you exhausted a card this turn.
[/list]

[b]New:[/b] Power Through — 1-cost Uncommon Skill. Gain 15/20 Block and add 2 Wounds to your hand.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Silent[/h1]

[b]Buffs[/b]
[list]
[*]Untouchable: Block 6/9 to 7/10.
[/list]

[b]Nerfs[/b]
[list]
[*]Corrosive Wave: drawing a card applies 2/3 Poison to one random enemy instead of all enemies.
[/list]

[b]Mechanics[/b]
[list]
[*]Master Planner: cost 2/1 to 1/0. Only the first non-Sly Skill played each turn gains Sly.
[*]Calculated Gamble: the upgrade removes Exhaust and no longer grants Retain. Wraith Form gains Innate, Retain, and Sly.
[/list]

[b]New:[/b] Concentrate — 0-cost Uncommon Skill. Discard 3/2 cards and gain 2 energy.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Defect[/h1]

[b]Buffs[/b]
[list]
[*]Glacier: Block 6/9 to 7/10. Charge Battery: Block 7/10 to 8/11.
[*]Defragment: Rare to Uncommon. Biased Cognition gains Innate and Retain.
[/list]

[b]Nerfs[/b]
[list]
[*]Flak Cannon: damage 8/11 to 7/10. Thunder: bonus damage 8/11 to 6/8.
[/list]

[b]Mechanics[/b]
[list]
[*]Darkness: triggers Dark Orb passives once. The base card has Exhaust; the upgrade does not.
[*]Hotfix: grants 2/3 temporary Focus; its cost increases by 1 for the rest of the turn after each play.
[*]Glasswork: increases all Glass Orb counters by 1/2 instead of granting Block.
[*]Modded: its cost increase lasts only until the end of the turn instead of the entire combat.
[/list]

[b]Reworks[/b]
[list]
[*]Consuming Shadow: gain Block equal to 50%/100% of a Dark Orb's unblocked damage when you evoke it.
[*]Coolant: playing an Attack channels a temporary Frost Orb that lasts until your next turn.
[*]Recycle: exhaust a card from your hand and gain energy equal to its current cost.
[*]Fuel: draw 1/2 cards instead of gaining 1/2 energy.
[*]Ice Lance: evoke all Frost Orbs; for each, deal 6/9 damage to a random enemy.
[*]Lightning Rod: evoke all Lightning Orbs; for each, gain 2/3 Thorns.
[*]Refract: when about to take damage, evoke a Glass Orb with a positive counter to prevent that damage.
[/list]

[b]New:[/b] Recursion and Heat Sinks.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Regent[/h1]

[b]Buffs[/b]
[list]
[*]Resonance: Star cost 2 to 1.
[*]Terraforming: Uncommon to Common. Neutron Aegis: Rare to Uncommon and Star cost 5 to 4.
[/list]

[b]Nerfs[/b]
[list]
[*]Collision Course: damage 10/14 to 9/13. Cosmic Indifference: Block 6/9 to 5/8.
[*]Decisions, Decisions: draw 3/5 to 2/4 cards.
[*]Parry: Block 10/14 to 9/13. Manifest Authority: Block 7/8 to 6/6.
[*]Arsenal: cost 1 to 2.
[/list]

[b]Mixed stat changes[/b]
[list]
[*]Dying Star: damage 9/11 to 12/16; Strength reduction 9/11 to 4/6.
[/list]

[b]Mechanics[/b]
[list]
[*]Know Thy Place: the upgrade applies 2 Weak and 2 Vulnerable instead of 1 each, and retains Exhaust.
[*]Glow: immediate draw 1 to 2; rarity Common to Uncommon.
[*]Hidden Cache: Stars gained now 1 to 2, next turn 3/4 to 2/3. Total unchanged.
[*]Furnace: upgraded Forge per turn 7 to 5, but it auto-plays at combat start.
[*]Bundle of Joy: the upgrade creates 3 upgraded Colorless cards instead of 4 unupgraded ones.
[/list]

[b]Reworks[/b]
[list]
[*]Gamma Blast: exhaust all Colorless cards in your hand; for each, deal 6/9 damage to all enemies.
[*]Conqueror: Sovereign Blade gains Replay 1 this turn instead of dealing double damage to one enemy.
[*]Comet: deal 18/23 damage, then gain 5 Stars.
[*]Fated Victory: convert your current Vigor into an equal amount of Strength.
[*]Genesis: card plays do not spend Stars this turn; after this turn, you cannot gain Stars for the rest of combat.
[*]Devastate: deal 10/14 damage and initially gain 1 Star. Fatal permanently increases this card's Star gain by 1 for the run.
[/list]
