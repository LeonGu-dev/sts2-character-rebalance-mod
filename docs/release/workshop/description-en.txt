[h1]Character Rebalance[/h1]

Rebalances selected Ironclad, Silent, Defect, Regent, and Necrobinder cards in Slay the Spire 2, adjusting costs, stats, rarities, and mechanics. Supports English and Simplified Chinese card text.

[h1]Changes at a glance[/h1]

Includes [b]83 card changes[/b]:

[list]
[*][b]Ironclad: 17[/b]. Changes to Exhaust, Strength, and Block cards; adds Power Through.
[*][b]Silent: 6[/b]. Changes to draw, Poison, and Sly cards; adds Concentrate.
[*][b]Defect: 19[/b]. Changes to Orbs, Focus, and Power cards; adds Recursion and Heat Sinks.
[*][b]Regent: 21[/b]. Changes to Stars, Forge, and Sovereign Blade cards, including reworks of Conqueror, Genesis, and Devastate.
[*][b]Necrobinder: 20[/b]. Reworks around Osty's deaths and revivals, Summon, Souls, and Doom. Soul draws when Exhausted, with Ethereal draws resolving after hand discard. Basic in-game testing is complete.
[/list]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]

[list]
[*]Requires [b]BaseLib 3.4.5 or later[/b].
[*]Supported game version: [b]v0.111.0[/b].
[/list]
